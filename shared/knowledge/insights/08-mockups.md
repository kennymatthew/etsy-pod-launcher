## Why Mockups Matter

**Evergreen**

"The mock up image is just as important as the design you make. This is what displays it for sale and actually makes the customer want to buy it. Otherwise it's just a design file. If you don't get the mockup right, your product is never going to sell. And I mean that it'll absolutely never sell if you use bad mockups."

Mockup creation is the final step before publishing. By this point designs should already be uploaded to Printify.

## How to Research Winning Mockups

**Etsy-specific tactic** | **Check** (mockup trends evolve — re-research each niche)

Don't pick mockups based on personal taste. Use data to find what's already proven to convert.

### Step-by-step research process
1. Search Etsy for the product + niche (e.g. "save the date shirt")
2. Open eRank → Listing View — this overlays an **outlier score** on each listing, showing how many times better it sells vs. other listings in the same shop
3. Look for listings with high outlier scores (e.g. 7×, 30×, 110×) — pay attention to what mockup style those listings use
4. Also check your existing competitors list and note what mockups those shops are using
5. Once you identify a mockup style that's working, search Etsy directly for that mockup (e.g. "comfort colors moss mockup") — the same mockup pack is usually for sale
6. The top result is often the exact mockup you just saw performing well — buy it ($3–$5 typical)

### Alternative: eRank database search
- Search eRank's Listing View database for broad terms (e.g. "comfort colors mockup")
- Switch to visual browse mode
- Sort by total sales or monthly sales
- Quickly identifies top-performing mockup styles without scrolling Etsy manually

## What Makes a Good Mockup

**Evergreen**

Four criteria — all must be true:

1. **Proven to sell** — verified via outlier score or sales data, not gut feel
2. **Matches Etsy's handmade vibe** — looks authentic and custom, not corporate or automated
3. **Bright, centered, not cluttered** — just the product + human or 1–2 accessory items; no junk filling the frame
4. **Vibe matches the design** — the aesthetic of the mockup must complement the aesthetic of the design

"Your design and mockup combination has to create a compelling listing that converts shoppers into buyers."

## What to Avoid

**Evergreen**

### Placeit — do not use
"You'll never find best selling listings using something like a Placeit mockup... absolutely no best selling listings on Etsy use mockups from Placeit."

Placeit mockups look clean to beginners but are immediately identifiable as non-handmade. Alek's test: "Try and find listings on Etsy that are selling print on demand products that are using a Placeit mockup. You won't be able to do it."

Buy custom mockup packs from Etsy sellers instead — they're already proven, cheap, and look authentic.

## How to Apply Your Design to a Mockup

### Manual method (Kittl)
1. Drag mockup image into Kittl
2. Scale to minimum 2000px wide (Etsy minimum resolution)
3. Drag design file on top → right-click → bring to front
4. Scale and position on the shirt
5. Select all layers → group (Cmd+G) → quick export

### Batch/automation methods (recommended for volume)
Three options depending on tools available:

| Method | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Figma | Free | Create component from design, use variants to apply to multiple mockups at once |
| Photoshop script | Adobe subscription | Fastest for large batches |
| Dynamic Mockups | Paid subscription | Purpose-built for POD mockup automation |

Always make multiple mockups per design so you can test which one performs best as the main listing image.

## Listing Photo Slots (Etsy)

**Etsy-specific**

Use 5–10 listing photos. Recommended set:

| Slot | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 (main) | Best-performing mockup — this is the scroll-stopper |
| 2–3 | Additional mockup styles (flat lay, lifestyle, close-up) |
| 4 | Different colorway or angle |
| 5–6 | Extra design variants (e.g. different name combos for personalized products) |
| 7 | **Color chart** — all available colors with names |
| 8 | **Sizing guide** — with measurements, kept consistent across all listings |
| 9+ | Video if possible — listings with video convert ~2× better, only ~28% of sellers use them |

⚠️ Keep slots 7 and 8 (color chart + sizing guide) in the same slots across every listing. Makes bulk updating easier.
