## Evergreen niches (no timing constraint)

These sell year-round — no seasonal start-by date applies:

- Occupation identity (nurse, teacher, firefighter, mechanic…)
- Personalised family gifts (custom name, birth year, kids names)
- Hobby identity (fishing, golf, gaming, hiking…)
- Pet owner (dog mom, cat dad…)
- Milestone (retirement, graduation, anniversary)

## How to use

1. Identify if your niche is seasonal or evergreen.
2. If seasonal: check if today is before the "Start by" date.
   - Yes → proceed to Phase 1.
   - No → switch to an evergreen niche OR accept reduced ranking this cycle and start immediately.
3. If evergreen → no timing check needed, proceed to Phase 1.
